# Arete — ἀρετή

Arete takes its name from the ancient Greek ideal of excellence: becoming your best through discipline, balance and practice. Built for athletes who train both endurance and strength, it brings your Garmin and Strava activities, strength sessions, recovery and goals into one place. Plan your next challenge, track what you actually complete and see how your fitness develops over time.

Your AI coach helps turn that history into direction, with daily briefings, post-session feedback and conversations grounded in your goals, preferences and constraints. Enable the optional RPG experience to meet Chiron, your mentor, and give your training a hero’s journey: choose an athlete class, earn experience and unlock new appearances as you progress. The pursuit is yours—build strength, respect recovery and keep working toward your own arete.
